# Selected Architecture

A-RD is selected. Its high-popularity branch can use researcher-0010, and a localized component can use researcher-0002, but their exponents are not averaged. The remaining low-tail branch is precisely RDθ. Once RDθ supplies D=Oθ(d), the total normalized algebraic and peeling loss is below `(D/p)²+3D/p`, which the near-linear trigger absorbs. Simple-root KTZ lifting gives one polynomial per component. Because every component polynomial satisfies `B(X,Y,Q)=0`, each `Z−Q` divides B; at most `deg_Z(B)≤D/d≤Kθ` distinct templates occur. Pigeonholing therefore returns one polynomial rather than merely a list.

# Limitations

This is an honest conditional theorem, not an unconditional benchmark improvement. RDθ remains unproved, the integrated reduction is newly synthesized rather than verifier-accepted, and exact exponent one is false. The statement is only for prime fields, dimension two and integer 100<d<p. Near d=p it may be vacuous through its constants, and no computational experiment is used as proof.
